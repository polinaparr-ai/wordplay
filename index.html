Using Wordplay

Enter your board, add up to seven rack letters, then find moves. Click a result once to preview it, then click the same result again to place it on the board. On phones, use the Board / Results tabs; selecting a result shows its preview on the board, and Place word confirms it. Use the arrows below the list for more results. Use ? for a blank tile (worth zero points).

Switch between Scoring (all legal moves) and Amusing (funny and rude words together). Both modes stay in descending points order. The defensive slider highlights a tactical suggestion among the active mode’s 100 highest-scoring placements, without changing points order.

The built-in ENABLE1 lexicon plus verified additions has 168,553 words of 2–15 letters, compiled by Alan Beale and M. Leo Cooper and released into the public domain. It is a general word-game list, not a current official Collins or NASPA tournament dictionary. The solver exhaustively checks the active list, including all feasible blank assignments. Dictionary source and credits. Main words and cross-words must be in that list. Scoring includes premium squares, cross-words and the 50-point seven-tile bonus. Tactical ranking = points + slider weight × (rack quality − board risk). Rack quality rewards a blank, S and balanced vowels/consonants, and penalises duplicates, extreme imbalance and Q without U or a blank. Board risk estimates newly opened straight-line routes to bonus squares within seven spaces, credits removed routes, and penalises the strongest legal one-tile extension found in the active dictionary. Bonus routes are geometric possibilities, not validated full-word replies. Extension replies are scored and cross-checked, but require the opponent to hold the stated letter. This is not a full opponent search, trained equity model, or win-probability estimate.

Existing tiles can be edited with the keyboard. Select an occupied square and use Toggle blank to switch its value between zero and the normal letter value. Opponent words are case-insensitive; mark blanks separately using their 1-based positions in the full word. Board and rack save in this browser. “Load example” restores a sample position; “Clear” starts an empty board.
